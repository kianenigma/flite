18//! # Recovery Pallet
19//!
20//! - [`Config`]
21//! - [`Call`]
22//!
23//! ## Overview
24//!
25//! The Recovery pallet is an M-of-N social recovery tool for users to gain
26//! access to their accounts if the private key or other authentication mechanism
27//! is lost. Through this pallet, a user is able to make calls on-behalf-of another
28//! account which they have recovered. The recovery process is protected by trusted
29//! "friends" whom the original account owner chooses. A threshold (M) out of N
30//! friends are needed to give another account access to the recoverable account.
31//!
32//! ### Recovery Configuration
33//!
34//! The recovery process for each recoverable account can be configured by the account owner.
35//! They are able to choose:
36//! * `friends` - The list of friends that the account owner trusts to protect the recovery process
37//!   for their account.
38//! * `threshold` - The number of friends that need to approve a recovery process for the account to
39//!   be successfully recovered.
40//! * `delay_period` - The minimum number of blocks after the beginning of the recovery process that
41//!   need to pass before the account can be successfully recovered.
42//!
43//! There is a configurable deposit that all users need to pay to create a recovery
44//! configuration. This deposit is composed of a base deposit plus a multiplier for
45//! the number of friends chosen. This deposit is returned in full when the account
46//! owner removes their recovery configuration.
47//!
48//! ### Recovery Life Cycle
49//!
50//! The intended life cycle of a successful recovery takes the following steps:
51//! 1. The account owner calls `create_recovery` to set up a recovery configuration for their
52//!    account.
53//! 2. At some later time, the account owner loses access to their account and wants to recover it.
54//!    Likely, they will need to create a new account and fund it with enough balance to support the
55//!    transaction fees and the deposit for the recovery process.
56//! 3. Using this new account, they call `initiate_recovery`.
57//! 4. Then the account owner would contact their configured friends to vouch for the recovery
58//!    attempt. The account owner would provide their old account id and the new account id, and
59//!    friends would call `vouch_recovery` with those parameters.
60//! 5. Once a threshold number of friends have vouched for the recovery attempt, the account owner
61//!    needs to wait until the delay period has passed, starting when they initiated the recovery
62//!    process.
63//! 6. Now the account owner is able to call `claim_recovery`, which subsequently allows them to
64//!    call `as_recovered` and directly make calls on-behalf-of the lost account.
65//! 7. Using the now recovered account, the account owner can call `close_recovery` on the recovery
66//!    process they opened, reclaiming the recovery deposit they placed.
67//! 8. Then the account owner should then call `remove_recovery` to remove the recovery
68//!    configuration on the recovered account and reclaim the recovery configuration deposit they
69//!    placed.
70//! 9. Using `as_recovered`, the account owner is able to call any other pallets to clean up their
71//!    state and reclaim any reserved or locked funds. They can then transfer all funds from the
72//!    recovered account to the new account.
73//! 10. When the recovered account becomes reaped (i.e. its free and reserved balance drops to
74//!     zero), the final recovery link is removed.
75//!
76//! ### Malicious Recovery Attempts
77//!
78//! Initializing the recovery process for a recoverable account is open and
79//! permissionless. However, the recovery deposit is an economic deterrent that
80//! should disincentivize would-be attackers from trying to maliciously recover
81//! accounts.
82//!
83//! The recovery deposit can always be claimed by the account which is trying
84//! to be recovered. In the case of a malicious recovery attempt, the account
85//! owner who still has access to their account can claim the deposit and
86//! essentially punish the malicious user.
87//!
88//! Furthermore, the malicious recovery attempt can only be successful if the
89//! attacker is also able to get enough friends to vouch for the recovery attempt.
90//! In the case where the account owner prevents a malicious recovery process,
91//! this pallet makes it near-zero cost to re-configure the recovery settings and
92//! remove/replace friends who are acting inappropriately.
93//!
94//! ### Safety Considerations
95//!
96//! It is important to note that this is a powerful pallet that can compromise the
97//! security of an account if used incorrectly. Some recommended practices for users
98//! of this pallet are:
99//!
100//! * Configure a significant `delay_period` for your recovery process: As long as you have access
101//!   to your recoverable account, you need only check the blockchain once every `delay_period`
102//!   blocks to ensure that no recovery attempt is successful against your account. Using off-chain
103//!   notification systems can help with this, but ultimately, setting a large `delay_period` means
104//!   that even the most skilled attacker will need to wait this long before they can access your
105//!   account.
106//! * Use a high threshold of approvals: Setting a value of 1 for the threshold means that any of
107//!   your friends would be able to recover your account. They would simply need to start a recovery
108//!   process and approve their own process. Similarly, a threshold of 2 would mean that any 2
109//!   friends could work together to gain access to your account. The only way to prevent against
110//!   these kinds of attacks is to choose a high threshold of approvals and select from a diverse
111//!   friend group that would not be able to reasonably coordinate with one another.
112//! * Reset your configuration over time: Since the entire deposit of creating a recovery
113//!   configuration is returned to the user, the only cost of updating your recovery configuration
114//!   is the transaction fees for the calls. Thus, it is strongly encouraged to regularly update
115//!   your recovery configuration as your life changes and your relationship with new and existing
116//!   friends change as well.
117//!
118//! ## Interface
119//!
120//! ### Dispatchable Functions
121//!
122//! #### For General Users
123//!
124//! * `create_recovery` - Create a recovery configuration for your account and make it recoverable.
125//! * `initiate_recovery` - Start the recovery process for a recoverable account.
126//!
127//! #### For Friends of a Recoverable Account
128//! * `vouch_recovery` - As a `friend` of a recoverable account, vouch for a recovery attempt on the
129//!   account.
130//!
131//! #### For a User Who Successfully Recovered an Account
132//!
133//! * `claim_recovery` - Claim access to the account that you have successfully completed the
134//!   recovery process for.
135//! * `as_recovered` - Send a transaction as an account that you have recovered. See other functions
136//!   below.
137//!
138//! #### For the Recoverable Account
139//!
140//! * `close_recovery` - Close an active recovery process for your account and reclaim the recovery
141//!   deposit.
142//! * `remove_recovery` - Remove the recovery configuration from the account, making it
143//!   un-recoverable.
144//!
145//! #### For Super Users
146//!
147//! * `set_recovered` - The ROOT origin is able to skip the recovery process and directly allow one
148//!   account to access another.

192/// An active recovery process.
193#[derive(Clone, Eq, PartialEq, Encode, Decode, Default, RuntimeDebug, TypeInfo, MaxEncodedLen)]
194pub struct ActiveRecovery<BlockNumber, Balance, Friends> {
195	/// The block number when the recovery process started.
196	pub created: BlockNumber,
197	/// The amount held in reserve of the `depositor`,
198	/// to be returned once this recovery process is closed.
199	pub deposit: Balance,
200	/// The friends which have vouched so far. Always sorted.
201	pub friends: Friends,
202}

204/// Configuration for recovering an account.
205#[derive(Clone, Eq, PartialEq, Encode, Decode, Default, RuntimeDebug, TypeInfo, MaxEncodedLen)]
206pub struct RecoveryConfig<BlockNumber, Balance, Friends> {
207	/// The minimum number of blocks since the start of the recovery process before the account
208	/// can be recovered.
209	pub delay_period: BlockNumber,
210	/// The amount held in reserve of the `depositor`,
211	/// to be returned once this configuration is removed.
212	pub deposit: Balance,
213	/// The list of friends which can help recover an account. Always sorted.
214	pub friends: Friends,
215	/// The number of approving friends needed to recover an account.
216	pub threshold: u16,
217}

230	#[pallet::config]
231	pub trait Config: frame_system::Config {
232		/// The overarching event type.
233		type RuntimeEvent: From<Event<Self>> + IsType<<Self as frame_system::Config>::RuntimeEvent>;
234
235		/// Weight information for extrinsics in this pallet.
236		type WeightInfo: WeightInfo;
237
238		/// The overarching call type.
239		type RuntimeCall: Parameter
240			+ Dispatchable<RuntimeOrigin = Self::RuntimeOrigin, PostInfo = PostDispatchInfo>
241			+ GetDispatchInfo
242			+ From<frame_system::Call<Self>>;
243
244		/// Query the current block number.
245		///
246		/// Must return monotonically increasing values when called from consecutive blocks.
247		/// Can be configured to return either:
248		/// - the local block number of the runtime via `frame_system::Pallet`
249		/// - a remote block number, eg from the relay chain through `RelaychainDataProvider`
250		/// - an arbitrary value through a custom implementation of the trait
251		///
252		/// There is currently no migration provided to "hot-swap" block number providers and it may
253		/// result in undefined behavior when doing so. Parachains are therefore best off setting
254		/// this to their local block number provider if they have the pallet already deployed.
255		///
256		/// Suggested values:
257		/// - Solo- and Relay-chains: `frame_system::Pallet`
258		/// - Parachains that may produce blocks sparingly or only when needed (on-demand):
259		///   - already have the pallet deployed: `frame_system::Pallet`
260		///   - are freshly deploying this pallet: `RelaychainDataProvider`
261		/// - Parachains with a reliably block production rate (PLO or bulk-coretime):
262		///   - already have the pallet deployed: `frame_system::Pallet`
263		///   - are freshly deploying this pallet: no strong recommendation. Both local and remote
264		///     providers can be used. Relay provider can be a bit better in cases where the
265		///     parachain is lagging its block production to avoid clock skew.
266		type BlockNumberProvider: BlockNumberProvider;
267
268		/// The currency mechanism.
269		type Currency: ReservableCurrency<Self::AccountId>;
270
271		/// The base amount of currency needed to reserve for creating a recovery configuration.
272		///
273		/// This is held for an additional storage item whose value size is
274		/// `2 + sizeof(BlockNumber, Balance)` bytes.
275		#[pallet::constant]
276		type ConfigDepositBase: Get<BalanceOf<Self>>;
277
278		/// The amount of currency needed per additional user when creating a recovery
279		/// configuration.
280		///
281		/// This is held for adding `sizeof(AccountId)` bytes more into a pre-existing storage
282		/// value.
283		#[pallet::constant]
284		type FriendDepositFactor: Get<BalanceOf<Self>>;
285
286		/// The maximum amount of friends allowed in a recovery configuration.
287		///
288		/// NOTE: The threshold programmed in this Pallet uses u16, so it does
289		/// not really make sense to have a limit here greater than u16::MAX.
290		/// But also, that is a lot more than you should probably set this value
291		/// to anyway...
292		#[pallet::constant]
293		type MaxFriends: Get<u32>;
294
295		/// The base amount of currency needed to reserve for starting a recovery.
296		///
297		/// This is primarily held for deterring malicious recovery attempts, and should
298		/// have a value large enough that a bad actor would choose not to place this
299		/// deposit. It also acts to fund additional storage item whose value size is
300		/// `sizeof(BlockNumber, Balance + T * AccountId)` bytes. Where T is a configurable
301		/// threshold.
302		#[pallet::constant]
303		type RecoveryDeposit: Get<BalanceOf<Self>>;
304	}

324		/// A recovery process has been removed for an account.
325		RecoveryRemoved { lost_account: T::AccountId },
326	}

328	#[pallet::error]
329	pub enum Error<T> {
330		/// User is not allowed to make a call on behalf of this account
331		NotAllowed,
332		/// Threshold must be greater than zero
333		ZeroThreshold,
334		/// Friends list must be greater than zero and threshold
335		NotEnoughFriends,
336		/// Friends list must be less than max friends
337		MaxFriends,
338		/// Friends list must be sorted and free of duplicates
339		NotSorted,
340		/// This account is not set up for recovery
341		NotRecoverable,
342		/// This account is already set up for recovery
343		AlreadyRecoverable,
344		/// A recovery process has already started for this account
345		AlreadyStarted,
346		/// A recovery process has not started for this rescuer
347		NotStarted,
348		/// This account is not a friend who can vouch
349		NotFriend,
350		/// The friend must wait until the delay period to vouch for this recovery
351		DelayPeriod,
352		/// This user has already vouched for this recovery
353		AlreadyVouched,
354		/// The threshold for recovering this account has not been met
355		Threshold,
356		/// There are still active recovery attempts that need to be closed
357		StillActive,
358		/// This account is already set up for recovery
359		AlreadyProxy,
360		/// Some internal state is broken.
361		BadState,
362	}

396	#[pallet::call]
397	impl<T: Config> Pallet<T> {
398		/// Send a call through a recovered account.
399		///
400		/// The dispatch origin for this call must be _Signed_ and registered to
401		/// be able to make calls on behalf of the recovered account.
402		///
403		/// Parameters:
404		/// - `account`: The recovered account you want to make a call on-behalf-of.
405		/// - `call`: The call you want to make with the recovered account.
406		#[pallet::call_index(0)]
407		#[pallet::weight({
408			let dispatch_info = call.get_dispatch_info();
409			(
410				T::WeightInfo::as_recovered().saturating_add(dispatch_info.call_weight),
411				dispatch_info.class,
412			)})]
413		pub fn as_recovered(
414			origin: OriginFor<T>,
415			account: AccountIdLookupOf<T>,
416			call: Box<<T as Config>::RuntimeCall>,
417		) -> DispatchResult {
418			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;
419			let account = T::Lookup::lookup(account)?;
420			// Check `who` is allowed to make a call on behalf of `account`
421			let target = Self::proxy(&who).ok_or(Error::<T>::NotAllowed)?;
422			ensure!(target == account, Error::<T>::NotAllowed);
423			call.dispatch(frame_system::RawOrigin::Signed(account).into())
424				.map(|_| ())
425				.map_err(|e| e.error)
426		}
427
428		/// Allow ROOT to bypass the recovery process and set a rescuer account
429		/// for a lost account directly.
430		///
431		/// The dispatch origin for this call must be _ROOT_.
432		///
433		/// Parameters:
434		/// - `lost`: The "lost account" to be recovered.
435		/// - `rescuer`: The "rescuer account" which can call as the lost account.
436		#[pallet::call_index(1)]
437		#[pallet::weight(T::WeightInfo::set_recovered())]
438		pub fn set_recovered(
439			origin: OriginFor<T>,
440			lost: AccountIdLookupOf<T>,
441			rescuer: AccountIdLookupOf<T>,
442		) -> DispatchResult {
443			ensure_root(origin)?;
444			let lost = T::Lookup::lookup(lost)?;
445			let rescuer = T::Lookup::lookup(rescuer)?;
446			// Create the recovery storage item.
447			<Proxy<T>>::insert(&rescuer, &lost);
448			Self::deposit_event(Event::<T>::AccountRecovered {
449				lost_account: lost,
450				rescuer_account: rescuer,
451			});
452			Ok(())
453		}
454
455		/// Create a recovery configuration for your account. This makes your account recoverable.
456		///
457		/// Payment: `ConfigDepositBase` + `FriendDepositFactor` * #_of_friends balance
458		/// will be reserved for storing the recovery configuration. This deposit is returned
459		/// in full when the user calls `remove_recovery`.
460		///
461		/// The dispatch origin for this call must be _Signed_.
462		///
463		/// Parameters:
464		/// - `friends`: A list of friends you trust to vouch for recovery attempts. Should be
465		///   ordered and contain no duplicate values.
466		/// - `threshold`: The number of friends that must vouch for a recovery attempt before the
467		///   account can be recovered. Should be less than or equal to the length of the list of
468		///   friends.
469		/// - `delay_period`: The number of blocks after a recovery attempt is initialized that
470		///   needs to pass before the account can be recovered.
471		#[pallet::call_index(2)]
472		#[pallet::weight(T::WeightInfo::create_recovery(friends.len() as u32))]
473		pub fn create_recovery(
474			origin: OriginFor<T>,
475			friends: Vec<T::AccountId>,
476			threshold: u16,
477			delay_period: BlockNumberFromProviderOf<T>,
478		) -> DispatchResult {
479			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;
480			// Check account is not already set up for recovery
481			ensure!(!<Recoverable<T>>::contains_key(&who), Error::<T>::AlreadyRecoverable);
482			// Check user input is valid
483			ensure!(threshold >= 1, Error::<T>::ZeroThreshold);
484			ensure!(!friends.is_empty(), Error::<T>::NotEnoughFriends);
485			ensure!(threshold as usize <= friends.len(), Error::<T>::NotEnoughFriends);
486			let bounded_friends: FriendsOf<T> =
487				friends.try_into().map_err(|_| Error::<T>::MaxFriends)?;
488			ensure!(Self::is_sorted_and_unique(&bounded_friends), Error::<T>::NotSorted);
489			// Total deposit is base fee + number of friends * factor fee
490			let friend_deposit = T::FriendDepositFactor::get()
491				.checked_mul(&bounded_friends.len().saturated_into())
492				.ok_or(ArithmeticError::Overflow)?;
493			let total_deposit = T::ConfigDepositBase::get()
494				.checked_add(&friend_deposit)
495				.ok_or(ArithmeticError::Overflow)?;
496			// Reserve the deposit
497			T::Currency::reserve(&who, total_deposit)?;
498			// Create the recovery configuration
499			let recovery_config = RecoveryConfig {
500				delay_period,
501				deposit: total_deposit,
502				friends: bounded_friends,
503				threshold,
504			};
505			// Create the recovery configuration storage item
506			<Recoverable<T>>::insert(&who, recovery_config);
507
508			Self::deposit_event(Event::<T>::RecoveryCreated { account: who });
509			Ok(())
510		}
511
512		/// Initiate the process for recovering a recoverable account.
513		///
514		/// Payment: `RecoveryDeposit` balance will be reserved for initiating the
515		/// recovery process. This deposit will always be repatriated to the account
516		/// trying to be recovered. See `close_recovery`.
517		///
518		/// The dispatch origin for this call must be _Signed_.
519		///
520		/// Parameters:
521		/// - `account`: The lost account that you want to recover. This account needs to be
522		///   recoverable (i.e. have a recovery configuration).
523		#[pallet::call_index(3)]
524		#[pallet::weight(T::WeightInfo::initiate_recovery())]
525		pub fn initiate_recovery(
526			origin: OriginFor<T>,
527			account: AccountIdLookupOf<T>,
528		) -> DispatchResult {
529			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;
530			let account = T::Lookup::lookup(account)?;
531			// Check that the account is recoverable
532			ensure!(<Recoverable<T>>::contains_key(&account), Error::<T>::NotRecoverable);
533			// Check that the recovery process has not already been started
534			ensure!(
535				!<ActiveRecoveries<T>>::contains_key(&account, &who),
536				Error::<T>::AlreadyStarted
537			);
538			// Take recovery deposit
539			let recovery_deposit = T::RecoveryDeposit::get();
540			T::Currency::reserve(&who, recovery_deposit)?;
541			// Create an active recovery status
542			let recovery_status = ActiveRecovery {
543				created: T::BlockNumberProvider::current_block_number(),
544				deposit: recovery_deposit,
545				friends: Default::default(),
546			};
547			// Create the active recovery storage item
548			<ActiveRecoveries<T>>::insert(&account, &who, recovery_status);
549			Self::deposit_event(Event::<T>::RecoveryInitiated {
550				lost_account: account,
551				rescuer_account: who,
552			});
553			Ok(())
554		}

556		/// Allow a "friend" of a recoverable account to vouch for an active recovery
557		/// process for that account.
558		///
559		/// The dispatch origin for this call must be _Signed_ and must be a "friend"
560		/// for the recoverable account.
561		///
562		/// Parameters:
563		/// - `lost`: The lost account that you want to recover.
564		/// - `rescuer`: The account trying to rescue the lost account that you want to vouch for.
565		///
566		/// The combination of these two parameters must point to an active recovery
567		/// process.
568		#[pallet::call_index(4)]
569		#[pallet::weight(T::WeightInfo::vouch_recovery(T::MaxFriends::get()))]
570		pub fn vouch_recovery(
571			origin: OriginFor<T>,
572			lost: AccountIdLookupOf<T>,
573			rescuer: AccountIdLookupOf<T>,
574		) -> DispatchResult {
575			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;
576			let lost = T::Lookup::lookup(lost)?;
577			let rescuer = T::Lookup::lookup(rescuer)?;
578			// Get the recovery configuration for the lost account.
579			let recovery_config = Self::recovery_config(&lost).ok_or(Error::<T>::NotRecoverable)?;
580			// Get the active recovery process for the rescuer.
581			let mut active_recovery =
582				Self::active_recovery(&lost, &rescuer).ok_or(Error::<T>::NotStarted)?;
583			// Make sure the voter is a friend
584			ensure!(Self::is_friend(&recovery_config.friends, &who), Error::<T>::NotFriend);
585			// Either insert the vouch, or return an error that the user already vouched.
586			match active_recovery.friends.binary_search(&who) {
587				Ok(_pos) => return Err(Error::<T>::AlreadyVouched.into()),
588				Err(pos) => active_recovery
589					.friends
590					.try_insert(pos, who.clone())
591					.map_err(|_| Error::<T>::MaxFriends)?,
592			}
593			// Update storage with the latest details
594			<ActiveRecoveries<T>>::insert(&lost, &rescuer, active_recovery);
595			Self::deposit_event(Event::<T>::RecoveryVouched {
596				lost_account: lost,
597				rescuer_account: rescuer,
598				sender: who,
599			});
600			Ok(())
601		}
602
603		/// Allow a successful rescuer to claim their recovered account.
604		///
605		/// The dispatch origin for this call must be _Signed_ and must be a "rescuer"
606		/// who has successfully completed the account recovery process: collected
607		/// `threshold` or more vouches, waited `delay_period` blocks since initiation.
608		///
609		/// Parameters:
610		/// - `account`: The lost account that you want to claim has been successfully recovered by
611		///   you.
612		#[pallet::call_index(5)]
613		#[pallet::weight(T::WeightInfo::claim_recovery(T::MaxFriends::get()))]
614		pub fn claim_recovery(
615			origin: OriginFor<T>,
616			account: AccountIdLookupOf<T>,
617		) -> DispatchResult {
618			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;
619			let account = T::Lookup::lookup(account)?;
620			// Get the recovery configuration for the lost account
621			let recovery_config =
622				Self::recovery_config(&account).ok_or(Error::<T>::NotRecoverable)?;
623			// Get the active recovery process for the rescuer
624			let active_recovery =
625				Self::active_recovery(&account, &who).ok_or(Error::<T>::NotStarted)?;
626			ensure!(!Proxy::<T>::contains_key(&who), Error::<T>::AlreadyProxy);
627			// Make sure the delay period has passed
628			let current_block_number = T::BlockNumberProvider::current_block_number();
629			let recoverable_block_number = active_recovery
630				.created
631				.checked_add(&recovery_config.delay_period)
632				.ok_or(ArithmeticError::Overflow)?;
633			ensure!(recoverable_block_number <= current_block_number, Error::<T>::DelayPeriod);
634			// Make sure the threshold is met
635			ensure!(
636				recovery_config.threshold as usize <= active_recovery.friends.len(),
637				Error::<T>::Threshold
638			);
639			frame_system::Pallet::<T>::inc_consumers(&who).map_err(|_| Error::<T>::BadState)?;
640			// Create the recovery storage item
641			Proxy::<T>::insert(&who, &account);
642			Self::deposit_event(Event::<T>::AccountRecovered {
643				lost_account: account,
644				rescuer_account: who,
645			});
646			Ok(())
647		}
648
649		/// As the controller of a recoverable account, close an active recovery
650		/// process for your account.
651		///
652		/// Payment: By calling this function, the recoverable account will receive
653		/// the recovery deposit `RecoveryDeposit` placed by the rescuer.
654		///
655		/// The dispatch origin for this call must be _Signed_ and must be a
656		/// recoverable account with an active recovery process for it.
657		///
658		/// Parameters:
659		/// - `rescuer`: The account trying to rescue this recoverable account.
660		#[pallet::call_index(6)]
661		#[pallet::weight(T::WeightInfo::close_recovery(T::MaxFriends::get()))]
662		pub fn close_recovery(
663			origin: OriginFor<T>,
664			rescuer: AccountIdLookupOf<T>,
665		) -> DispatchResult {
666			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;
667			let rescuer = T::Lookup::lookup(rescuer)?;
668			// Take the active recovery process started by the rescuer for this account.
669			let active_recovery =
670				<ActiveRecoveries<T>>::take(&who, &rescuer).ok_or(Error::<T>::NotStarted)?;
671			// Move the reserved funds from the rescuer to the rescued account.
672			// Acts like a slashing mechanism for those who try to maliciously recover accounts.
673			let res = T::Currency::repatriate_reserved(
674				&rescuer,
675				&who,
676				active_recovery.deposit,
677				BalanceStatus::Free,
678			);
679			debug_assert!(res.is_ok());
680			Self::deposit_event(Event::<T>::RecoveryClosed {
681				lost_account: who,
682				rescuer_account: rescuer,
683			});
684			Ok(())
685		}
686
687		/// Remove the recovery process for your account. Recovered accounts are still accessible.
688		///
689		/// NOTE: The user must make sure to call `close_recovery` on all active
690		/// recovery attempts before calling this function else it will fail.
691		///
692		/// Payment: By calling this function the recoverable account will unreserve
693		/// their recovery configuration deposit.
694		/// (`ConfigDepositBase` + `FriendDepositFactor` * #_of_friends)
695		///
696		/// The dispatch origin for this call must be _Signed_ and must be a
697		/// recoverable account (i.e. has a recovery configuration).
698		#[pallet::call_index(7)]
699		#[pallet::weight(T::WeightInfo::remove_recovery(T::MaxFriends::get()))]
700		pub fn remove_recovery(origin: OriginFor<T>) -> DispatchResult {
701			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;
702			// Check there are no active recoveries
703			let mut active_recoveries = <ActiveRecoveries<T>>::iter_prefix_values(&who);
704			ensure!(active_recoveries.next().is_none(), Error::<T>::StillActive);
705			// Take the recovery configuration for this account.
706			let recovery_config = <Recoverable<T>>::take(&who).ok_or(Error::<T>::NotRecoverable)?;
707
708			// Unreserve the initial deposit for the recovery configuration.
709			T::Currency::unreserve(&who, recovery_config.deposit);
710			Self::deposit_event(Event::<T>::RecoveryRemoved { lost_account: who });
711			Ok(())
712		}
713
714		/// Cancel the ability to use `as_recovered` for `account`.
715		///
716		/// The dispatch origin for this call must be _Signed_ and registered to
717		/// be able to make calls on behalf of the recovered account.
718		///
719		/// Parameters:
720		/// - `account`: The recovered account you are able to call on-behalf-of.
721		#[pallet::call_index(8)]
722		#[pallet::weight(T::WeightInfo::cancel_recovered())]
723		pub fn cancel_recovered(
724			origin: OriginFor<T>,
725			account: AccountIdLookupOf<T>,
726		) -> DispatchResult {
727			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;
728			let account = T::Lookup::lookup(account)?;
729			// Check `who` is allowed to make a call on behalf of `account`
730			ensure!(Self::proxy(&who) == Some(account), Error::<T>::NotAllowed);
731			Proxy::<T>::remove(&who);
732
733			frame_system::Pallet::<T>::dec_consumers(&who);
734			Ok(())
735		}
736	}
737}
738
739impl<T: Config> Pallet<T> {
740	/// Check that friends list is sorted and has no duplicates.
741	fn is_sorted_and_unique(friends: &Vec<T::AccountId>) -> bool {
742		friends.windows(2).all(|w| w[0] < w[1])
743	}
744
745	/// Check that a user is a friend in the friends list.
746	fn is_friend(friends: &Vec<T::AccountId>, friend: &T::AccountId) -> bool {
747		friends.binary_search(&friend).is_ok()
748	}
749}
